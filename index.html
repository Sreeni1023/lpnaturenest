About the property

Built for the day you don't want to end.

LP Nature Nest is a red-roofed Manduva farm house set amid open green grounds in Bastepur village, Chevella — close enough for an easy drive, far enough that the city noise actually fades. The property was opened this year, and every corner of it, the hand-painted murals, the mandapam-style porch, the orchard rows, was built for people to gather in.

Whether it's a family day out, a birthday, a quiet weekend stay, or a full-blown celebration, the space is yours alone for the booking — no shared timings, no other groups.

Good to know

Our policy

Guest guidelines

Bookings are private to your group. Please look after the property and the grounds, supervise children near the pool, and keep the space clean during your stay. Any damage may incur additional charges.

Bringing your own beverages

Guests may bring their own beverages for consumption within the private premises, in line with applicable local laws. The property does not sell alcohol and isn't responsible for misuse or overconsumption.

Pets welcome

Well-behaved pets are welcome. Owners are responsible for their pets at all times and are requested to keep the grounds clean after them.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is the farmhouse completely private?

Yes — the entire property, pool and house are booked exclusively to your group. No other guests share the property during your visit.

Can we cook our own food?

Yes, the open kitchen is fully equipped with cookware, a gas stove, fridge and utensils — suitable for groups up to 30 for self-cooking.

Are pets and outside beverages allowed?

Both are welcome — see the "Our Policy" section above for the details on each.

How many guests can the property host?

The grounds comfortably host large day-out groups, with overnight stays for smaller groups across the two master bedrooms.

How do I book?

Message us on WhatsApp or call directly — we'll confirm your date, guest count and requirements, and share the booking details.
